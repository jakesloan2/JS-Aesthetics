Conditions

What’s bothering
you?

Most people arrive knowing what they see in the mirror, not which treatment fixes it. That’s the right way round.

Below are the concerns clients most often bring to the lounge, and the treatments that tend to help. Nothing here is a diagnosis — that comes from sitting down together.

Fine Lines & Wrinkles

Lines that used to appear only when you moved, and now stay put.

Anti-wrinkle injections, Profhilo, microneedling

Thin or Uneven Lips

Volume lost with age, or a natural asymmetry you’ve always noticed.

Lip filler, lip flip

Hooded & Tired Eyes

Heavy lids, crepey texture and hollows that make you look tired when you’re not.

Plasma fibroblast, polynucleotides

Acne Scarring

Pitting and texture left behind long after the breakouts stopped.

Microneedling, chemical peel

Pigmentation & Sun Damage

Patches and freckling that have deepened over the years.

Chemical peel, microneedling

Dull, Dehydrated Skin

Skin that’s lost its bounce and no longer catches the light.

Profhilo, dermaplaning, luxury facial

Enlarged Pores & Texture

Rough, uneven skin that makeup sits on top of rather than in.

Microneedling, dermaplaning

Nasolabial & Marionette Lines

The creases from nose to mouth, and mouth to chin, that deepen at rest.

Dermal filler

Jaw Clenching & Grinding

Waking with a sore jaw, headaches, or a squarer face than you’d like.

Masseter treatment

Neck Laxity

Slackness and banding through the neck and under the jaw.

Nefertiti neck lift, Profhilo

Excessive Sweating

Underarm sweating that dictates what you wear and where you sit.

Hyperhidrosis treatment

Sparse or Uneven Brows

Gaps, thinning ends, or brows that never quite match.

Henna, hybrid, lamination

JS Aesthetics Beauty Lounge is an aesthetics and beauty clinic in Bangor, County Down, Northern Ireland. Treatments include anti-wrinkle injections, lip filler and dermal filler, filler dissolving, Profhilo skin boosters, salmon DNA polynucleotides, PRP therapy, plasma fibroblast skin tightening, microneedling, chemical peels, LED light therapy, dermaplaning and luxury facials, vitamin B12 and biotin injections, henna and hybrid brows, brow lamination, Korean lash lift, lash and brow tinting, and waxing for women and men. Clients travel from across North Down and Ards including Newtownards, Holywood, Donaghadee, Groomsport, Comber, Helen’s Bay and Belfast.
