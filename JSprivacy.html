Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information JS Aesthetics Beauty Lounge collects, why, and what your rights are under UK GDPR.

Who we are

JS Aesthetics Beauty Lounge, 4 Lord Wardens Court, Bangor, County Down, BT19 1GJ. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us at Beautyandaestheticsbyjodie@hotmail.com or on +44 7889 178320.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. Your name, contact details and anything you choose to tell us when you message us by email, WhatsApp, social media or the contact form.
  • Appointments. Your name, contact details, appointment history and payment record.
  • Consultation records. For injectable and skin treatments we take a medical history, record the treatment given and note any aftercare advice. This is health information and is treated with additional care.
  • Photographs. Before and after images, only where you have given separate written consent, and only used publicly where you have specifically agreed to that.

Why we hold it, and our lawful basis

  • To answer your enquiry and arrange your appointment — taking steps at your request before entering a contract.
  • To treat you safely and keep an accurate clinical record — necessary for the provision of health care, and for our legitimate interest in defending any future claim.
  • To meet insurance and regulatory obligations — legal obligation.
  • To send marketing, where you have asked to receive it — consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to a booking are deleted after [PERIOD — commonly 12 months]. Treatment and consultation records are kept for [PERIOD — confirm with insurer] after your last appointment. Photographs are kept until you withdraw consent.

Who we share it with

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
