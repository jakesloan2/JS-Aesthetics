Jodie is an advanced aesthetic practitioner with years of experience in her field. She founded JS Aesthetics Beauty Lounge to do aesthetics the way she’d want them done herself — unhurried, conservative, and honest about results.

She works one client at a time from a private lounge in Bangor. No waiting room, no conveyor belt, no being handed to whoever is free. Your appointment is yours, and it isn’t squeezed between two others.

Aesthetics sit on one side of what she does; classic beauty on the other. Brows, lashes and waxing are held to exactly the same standard as the injectables — which is why most clients end up booking both.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes, for anything injectable. Jodie takes a medical history, assesses your facial structure and skin, and talks through what’s realistic before anything is agreed.

How long does lip filler last?

Usually six to twelve months, depending on the product, the amount placed and how quickly your body breaks it down. Some people metabolise filler noticeably faster than others.

Is there downtime after microneedling?

Expect redness like mild sunburn for 24 to 48 hours. Most people are comfortable bare-faced the next day and fully settled by day three.

How soon will anti-wrinkle injections work?

Movement starts to soften from around day three to five, with the full effect at two weeks — which is when a review appointment is worth having. Results typically last three to four months.

Is there anything that would stop me being treated?

Pregnancy and breastfeeding rule out most injectables, as do certain medical conditions, medications and active skin infections in the treatment area. Bring your full medical history and Jodie will advise.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Clearpay splits any treatment into four interest-free payments. You need to be 18 or over and Clearpay’s terms apply.
